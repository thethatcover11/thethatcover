Got questions?

We have answers.

THAT COVER is redefining the phone case experience. Instead of one permanent look, we offer interchangeable design cards. Simply slide a card into your clear case to instantly refresh your style. Switch them out anytime with zero tools and zero hassle.
Our 5 curated collections are: Cabin Collection (cozy, warm aesthetic), Divine (spiritual themes), Persona Collection (personality-driven designs), The Art Series (artistic illustrations), and Zariya- The Dhaga (thread-inspired, cultural aesthetics). Each collection has multiple designs to choose from.
We support iPhone, Samsung, OnePlus, Redmi, Realme and most popular Android models. Message us on Instagram with your exact phone model before ordering and we'll confirm availability instantly.
Basic cards ship in 4–7 days. Semi-custom in 5–8 days. Full custom illustrations in 7–10 days. We ship pan-India via tracked courier. You'll get a tracking link once dispatched.
Absolutely! Extra cards start from just ₹99. That's the whole point — buy more cards, swap your vibe whenever you want. One case can hold a whole wardrobe of cards.
Since each card is made-to-order, we don't accept returns. However, if your product arrives damaged or incorrect, we replace it immediately — no questions asked. Your satisfaction is everything to us.
